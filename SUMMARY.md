## RAIN.SG
[RAIN.SG (Responsible AI Network Singapore)](https://rain-sg.org) is a grassroots community for anyone thinking seriously about responsible AI in Singapore — researchers, builders, policymakers, and the AI-curious alike. Through regular sessions ranging from technical paper reading groups to informal chats, rain·sg brings people together across the city to explore how AI is built and governed, working toward a smarter, safer world one conversation at a time. 

Our name hidden in plain sight: R-AI-N, the Responsible AI Network Singapore. We're a grassroots community of researchers, builders, policymakers, and the AI-curious who meet regularly across Singapore to think seriously about how AI gets built and governed.

### What we run

* **Crossroad Chatters:** Small group conversations on where AI is actually headed, from "is this doomsday or tomorrow's reality" to the questions nobody has resolved yet. Informal by design.
* **RAIx Series:** Responsible AI crossed with the fields it is reshaping. We started with education, and more domains are added as the community chooses them.
* **Paper Trail:** A technical reading group working through AI safety papers and course material, session by session, designed for people who want to go deeper than a panel discussion.

## About Odysea
[Odysea](https://odysea-ai.org) is a free, 8-week pilot for anyone in SEA curious about AI alignment: weekday evenings, topics shaped by the group, sessions led by local TAs and inspired by Iliad's curriculum.

It is a lighter, more introductory take on Iliad's curriculum, shaped with input from individual Iliad instructors. Topics include Singular Learning Theory, Training Dynamics, Decision Theory and Reinforcement Learning.

Odysea is organised by RAIN.SG's technical arm.

### How to join

Both tracks run on the same weekly sessions and the same curriculum. The difference is structure, not content, and there's no cost either way.

* **Explorer (low commitment):** For anyone still figuring out their interest in AI safety. Drop into any individual session, with no attendance requirement, no penalty for missing a week and no capstone required.
* **Fellow (full commitment):** For those ready to commit to the full pilot. Express interest upfront, attend at least 75% of sessions, complete a capstone project and receive a certificate of completion.
